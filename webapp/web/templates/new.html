Welcome

Welcome to Sloppy Wealth calculator. This is an approximation tool to aid anyone seeking answers to things like "What will be my net worth in year YYYY?" or "When can I sell the house to not lose money on it?" or "Is it cheaper to buy or ro rent/lease?", etc.

Important notes

  • THIS IS NOT A FINANCIAL ADVISE
  • At not point in time is your data saved server-side.
  • Your data is TEMPORARILY stored in your CURRENT browser tab. No other tabs have access to that data
  • To preserve your data use Save to save file locally on your computer and subsequently use Load when you'd like to resume working on your data
  • This app is only a "calculator" and YOU will have to direct it and provide best data for best results
  • Results this app provides are "ballpark" figures usefull for planning but they will most definitely differ from real life
