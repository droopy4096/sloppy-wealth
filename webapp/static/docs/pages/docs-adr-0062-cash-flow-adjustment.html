ADR-0062: Adjustment of a cash flow's amount (CashFlowAdjustment)

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-11
  • Relates to: ADR-0012 (events, cash flows), ADR-0013 (indexing), ADR-0033 (revaluation of assets), ADR-0043 (renewals: last one in a month wins)

Context

Indexed amounts grow smoothly, but real bills do not: property tax can stay flat for years and then jump; a salary changes with a promotion. Today the only way is to end the item and start another one, which breaks when dates move. The domain model already has CashFlowAdjustment (a new amount on a date); the PoC has not built it. Asset values have marketAdjustment (ADR-0033), but only for properties.

Decision

  1. A new adjustment item names a cash flow (income, expense, contribution, or a withdrawal with method amount), a date and a new amount (with per, default the flow's). From that month the flow's amount is the new amount.
  2. marketAdjustment also accepts vehicles (ADR-0033 amendment): their value is set (percent or new value) and depreciation continues from it.

Mechanics (defaults; not separate owner decisions)

  • The new amount is in dollars of the adjustment date. Indexing continues with the flow's own indexing; its yearly steps count from the adjustment date.
  • Several adjustments of one flow follow each other; in one month the last in item order wins (as renewals, ADR-0043).
  • An adjustment outside the flow's window changes nothing until (unless) the flow runs.
  • Each adjustment appears in the event log in its month.
  • Not covered: pensions (their own indexing, ADR-0052), percent and annuity withdrawals (derived amounts), vehicle maintenance (an attribute of the vehicle; use an attached expense, ADR-0040 amendment, for a cost that needs resets).

Alternatives considered

  • One generic adjustment item for flows and asset values: mixes a flow's amount and an asset's value in one form; duplicates marketAdjustment.
  • Splitting the flow into dated pieces (no change): manual, breaks when dates move.

Consequences

  • Bills that change in steps are entered as one flow plus adjustments.
  • Older files are unchanged.

Amendment (2026-11-11): new rate for percent withdrawals

  • An adjustment of a withdrawal with method percent gives a new annualRate (fraction) instead of an amount. From the adjustment month the withdrawal takes that rate: its monthly amount is re-set then (opening balance × rate / 12, as at its start) and on each anniversary of the adjustment date.
  • Several follow each other; in one month the last in item order wins. Annuity withdrawals stay out of scope (derived amounts).
