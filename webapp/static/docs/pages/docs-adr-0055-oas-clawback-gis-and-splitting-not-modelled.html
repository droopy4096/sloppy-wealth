ADR-0055: OAS clawback, GIS and pension income splitting are not modelled

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-09
  • Relates to: ADR-0002 (one economic unit), ADR-0004 (tax seam), ADR-0017 (flat rate), ADR-0037 (retirement-income goals), ADR-0052 (pensions); CRITIQUE.md C-P9

Context

These rules depend on each person's income, and the model has one household and one flat rate. Without a record, the bias they cause stays invisible.

Decision

  • OAS is counted at its full amount, before the recovery tax (clawback: 15% of a person's net income above about $93k).
  • GIS and other income-tested benefits are not modelled.
  • Pension income splitting is not modelled. Its effect, if any, is assumed to be inside the user's chosen effective tax rate.
  • A later per-person tax rule can add them behind the jurisdiction seam (ADR-0004).

Consequences

  • Retirement income and goals (ADR-0037) are overstated when a person's income exceeds the clawback threshold (large RRIF withdrawals, DB pension), and understated for low-income households that would receive GIS.
  • The user should lower the OAS amount by hand when a clawback is expected.
