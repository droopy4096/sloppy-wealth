Walkthrough scenario: two houses to retirement fund

Used to test the domain model and pipeline end to end. The numbers are illustrative; ⚠ marks assumptions to confirm.

Facts (from the user)

  • Owns two houses today, each with a mortgage and an appreciation rate.
  • Sells both in 10 years. The proceeds go to the bank account, which becomes the retirement fund (finite).
  • Retires at 60, plans until 80. Question: does the fund cover the expected pension draw?

What the walkthrough shows

  • SaleEvent needs proceedsTo : Account (and PurchaseEvent needs paidFrom). Added to the class diagram, v0.3.
  • A sale triggers a settlement: pay off the secured mortgage (+ early closure penalty), capital gains tax (H2 only), remainder to the bank, and the property's costs and mortgage payments stop.
  • The order of steps within a month (Q-08) is decided in ADR-0018.
  • Income is entered net by default; taxable income (e.g. rent) is flagged (ADR-0020).
