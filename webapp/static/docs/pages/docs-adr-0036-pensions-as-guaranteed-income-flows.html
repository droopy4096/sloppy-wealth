ADR-0036: Guaranteed pensions are income cash flows; RRIF rules are not modelled

  • Status: Accepted; amended by ADR-0052 (pension value in net worth, retirement date)
  • Date: 2026-11-05

Context

Retirement money comes either as a finite pot (RRSP) or as guaranteed income until death (defined-benefit pension, CPP/OAS-like), usually indexed to inflation.

Decision

  • A pension is an income CashFlow from outside into an account. It starts at the pension start date, ends at the horizon end (the planned lifespan), is InflationIndexed (or CustomRate for partial indexing) and is flagged taxable (ADR-0020).
  • A pension has no holding and no value in net worth (no commuted value). Retirement options are compared with income tallies (ADR-0037), not net worth.
  • RRSP to RRIF conversion and minimum withdrawals are not modelled. The user is responsible for withdrawals large enough to meet the minimums.

Consequences

  • Net worth understates the wealth of someone who relies on a pension. This is a known limitation, and comparisons of retirement options should use income-based goals.
  • Watch items: RRIF minimums (Q-13), pension commuted value (Q-14), annuity purchase (Q-15).
