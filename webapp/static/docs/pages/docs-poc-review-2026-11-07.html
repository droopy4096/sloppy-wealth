PoC review: what the Python PoC changed in the planning (2026-11-07)

Scope: branch poc/python-flask (21 commits, 69734e3..049f16b), compared with the ADRs and the domain model on main (v0.16).

1. Confirmed by the PoC (no change needed)

  • Month order: income first, then events, then debits, then growth on opening balances (ADR-0018). No false shortfalls on payday.
  • Financed purchase (ADR-0025), mortgage secured by a property (ADR-0024), sale settles the secured loans (ADR-0034), selling costs (ADR-0031).
  • Derived loan payments; prepayments shorten the loan, the payment stays (ADR-0029).
  • Shared horizon for comparisons (ADR-0028).
  • Minimal input and crude modelling (ADR-0040): every PoC scenario ran with a handful of items.
  • Letting cash go negative was a useful diagnostic: it exposed the 2021 funding gap in the baseline. This supports reporting shortfalls with an amount and period (ADR-0015).

4. Gaps: registered funds and defined-benefit pensions

RRSP / TFSA (and non-registered investments)

In the model these are Accounts (ADR-0011) with a tax treatment (TFSA = tax-free, RRSP = tax-deferred), a return rate, an optional withdrawal tax rate (ADR-0035), contributions and withdrawals as cash flows, even drawdown (ADR-0039) and sweeps (ADR-0030). Liquidation net worth already deducts the tax embedded in RRSP balances (ADR-0031). Missing:

#GapNotes
R1RRSP contribution deduction: contributions reduce taxable income (a refund)Not modelled anywhere. With one flat rate: deduction = contribution × rate. Options: same month (simplest), or as an annual refund
R2Withdrawal as a rate (e.g. 4% a year of the balance)Only fixed amounts and even drawdown exist. Needs a percentage-of-balance withdrawal, recalculated yearly or monthly
R3Contribution level as an amount, possibly indexed, with an end dateAlready a CashFlow operating → account. Needs a contribution form in the authoring tool only
R4Where they show in the output ("normally listed as assets")In the model they are accounts, not assets. Output should group them as investments/registered funds within assets net worth
R5Employer matching (group RRSP), contribution roomRoom is per person (Q-06, watch item); matching is a second cash flow from outside
R6Interaction with income-less mode (ADR-0044)Are contributions "covered from outside" (they still land in the account) or skipped? Withdrawals are income: ignored or kept?
R7RRIF minimumsQ-13, watch item

Defined-benefit pension (guaranteed until death, inflation-indexed)

ADR-0036: an income cash flow from the pension start to the horizon end (death), inflation-indexed, taxable, no value in net worth. The PoC can already express it as an income item (indexing = inflation, taxable). Missing or to decide:

#GapNotes
D1A dedicated pension type in authoring (start date, amount, indexing; end = horizon)Authoring only; the engine needs nothing new
D2Value in net worth (commuted value)Q-14. Without it, "assets net worth" understates someone relying on a pension
D3Income-less mode ignores it (ADR-0044 ignores all income)A retirement scenario in that mode would drop the pension. Needs a rule: e.g. income-less mode ignores employment income only
D4Member contributions while working, bridge benefit to 65, survivor benefitContributions are an expense or reduce free income; bridge = a second, ending income flow; survivor benefit out of scope (single economic unit, ADR-0002)
D5Partial indexing (e.g. CPI capped at 2%)CustomRate covers a fixed partial rate; a cap would need a new rate generator (Q-11 CompositeRate)
