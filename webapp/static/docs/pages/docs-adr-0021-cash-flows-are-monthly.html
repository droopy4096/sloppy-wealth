ADR-0021: Cash flows are monthly; annual amounts are spread evenly

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Some costs are naturally annual (maintenance, insurance, property tax). We had to choose between adding a frequency to cash flows and spreading annual amounts over the months.

Decision

  • Every cash flow is monthly. There is no frequency attribute.
  • Annual amounts and annual percentage rates are spread evenly: monthly amount = annual / 12. Authoring tooling may accept annual input and convert it.

Alternatives considered

  • A frequency field (MONTHLY / ANNUAL plus a month of occurrence): more realistic cash timing, but more model and more stepping logic.

Consequences

  • Spreading is gentle on cash timing, so shortfalls caused by a lumpy annual bill are not visible. Real lump-sum items can still be entered as one-off events.
  • Frequency can be added later as a new attribute with default MONTHLY, without breaking existing scenarios.

Amendment (2026-11-07): amounts may be stated per month, quarter or year

  • A cash flow states its amount per month (default), per quarter or per year (per). The stated figure is kept in the JSON, so files show contract figures such as $120,000 a year and stay easy to edit (ADR-0008).
  • The engine still works monthly: a quarterly amount is applied as 1/3 and a yearly one as 1/12 every month, each monthly movement rounded (ADR-0009). Indexing applies to the stated figure.
  • Quarterly and yearly amounts are spread evenly, not paid in one go. A "paid in one go" option (e.g. a bonus every March) can be added later if cash timing within a quarter or year matters.
  • No other frequencies (weekly, bi-weekly) (ADR-0007).
  • This replaces "there is no frequency attribute" above; conversion is no longer left to authoring tools.
