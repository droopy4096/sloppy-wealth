ADR-0027: Tallies, and goals evaluated every month by the stepping stage

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04
  • Refines: ADR-0010 (goal observation moves into the monthly stepping stage), ADR-0014 (goal conditions are expressed over tallies)

Context

The rent vs buy break-even goal needs running quantities that are not plain holding balances, e.g. the owner's net gain and the cumulative rent paid. The user wants goals to be evaluated each month by the executor of the schedule, attached to objects that keep their own running tallies.

Decision

  • A Tally is a named running quantity that the monthly stepping stage updates once per period, after the period closes. Its value for each period is stored in that period's closed state.
  • Kinds of tally (extensible):
    • HoldingMetric: VALUE, BALANCE, COST_BASIS, SHORTFALL of one holding (ADR-0014).
    • NetWorth: total, or liquidation-adjusted (see R3).
    • TagSum: cumulative movements carrying a tag, filtered by direction (e.g. all outflows tagged housing:buy).
    • Derived: a formula over other tallies, e.g. ownerNetGain = (VALUE(home) − BALANCE(mortgage)) − TagSum(housing:buy, outflows).
  • A Goal is attached to one or more tallies and evaluates its condition every month, as the last step of the period (new step 9 after close in ADR-0018). REACH and MAINTAIN semantics are unchanged (ADR-0014).
  • Global and local goals (ADR-0014) become special cases: a global goal is attached to a NetWorth tally, a local goal to a HoldingMetric tally.

Consequences

  • ADR-0010's post-stepping "goal observation" stage only collects goal outcomes into the result.
  • Tallies "update their internal value" conceptually, but the value is part of each closed period's state, so stages stay pure (ADR-0010) and the state stays serialisable (ADR-0008).
  • Tallies may only read the current closed period and earlier ones. Derived tallies are evaluated in dependency order, with no cycles.

Resolved open points

  • Cross-scenario tallies: scenarios are run in lockstep as a set, and goals may attach to tallies from any scenario in the set (ADR-0028).
  • Goals only observe: a goal decides met or not met (or held or violated), and the engine reports the period. Goals never trigger actions. ADR-0006 stands; conditional or reactive events are out of scope.
