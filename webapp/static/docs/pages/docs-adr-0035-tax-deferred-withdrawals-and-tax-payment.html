ADR-0035: Taxing tax-deferred withdrawals, and which account pays tax

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-05
  • Amends: ADR-0017 (single flat rate), ADR-0018 step 7

Context

RRSP withdrawals are taxed, often in a lower bracket than working income. The tax step deducted tax without naming the account that pays it.

Decision

  • A tax-deferred account may specify its own withdrawalTaxRate : RateGenerator [0..1]. If it is not set, the scenario's effective tax rate applies (ADR-0017).
  • Withdrawal cash flows are gross: the stated amount leaves the account, and tax on it is charged separately in the tax step. The engine does not gross amounts up; if a net amount is wanted, the user enters the grossed-up figure.
  • The scenario has taxPaidFrom : Account. Every tax computed in the tax step (and the capital gains tax on a sale) is debited from it, under the capping and shortfall rule (ADR-0015).

Consequences

  • ADR-0017's "one flat rate" becomes "one flat rate by default, overridable for each tax-deferred account".
  • A shortfall on taxPaidFrom signals that the plan cannot afford its taxes.
