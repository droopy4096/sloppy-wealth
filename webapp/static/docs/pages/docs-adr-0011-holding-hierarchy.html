ADR-0011: Accounts, assets and liabilities share a common Holding base

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Accounts (TFSA, RRSP, cash), assets (real estate, vehicles) and liabilities (mortgage) all carry a value that changes each period through rates and movements. Net worth, comparisons and goals all aggregate across them.

Decision

  • A common base concept, Holding, has specialised subtypes:
    • Account: holds money, has a tax treatment, and takes contributions, withdrawals and returns.
    • Asset: a non-cash value with appreciation or depreciation, plus fixed and variable costs.
    • Liability: a negative value, with interest, a payment schedule, prepayments and penalties.
  • Net worth is the sum over all holdings.

Alternatives considered

  • Separate, unrelated types: every aggregation and every rate-application step would have to be duplicated for each type.

Consequences

  • The monthly stepping steps and goal observation can work on holdings in general and specialise only where needed.
  • A goal such as "retirement funds ≥ $1M" needs a way to select holdings (by subtype, tax treatment or tag). Selectors are to be designed in the domain model.
