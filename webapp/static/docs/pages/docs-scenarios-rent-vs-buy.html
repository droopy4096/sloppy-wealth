Decisions taken from this scenario

R1 → ADR-0029, R2 → ADR-0030, R3 → ADR-0031, R4 → ADR-0028, R5 → ADR-0032, break-even → ADR-0027 + ADR-0028.

Assumptions: closing costs and the CMHC premium are entered amounts, not calculated by tax rules. TFSA contribution limits are not enforced (Q-06).

Break-even goal (requested, being defined)

"Report when owning has paid off compared with renting": e.g. thanks to appreciation, after about 2 years the owner is ahead of what rent would have cost. Principal residence, so no tax on the gain (ADR-0017).

Candidate definition, per period P (all cumulative from the purchase):

  • owner's net gain G(P) = (home value − mortgage balance) − cash paid out on housing:buy (down payment, closing costs, mortgage payments, costs) = appreciation − (interest + property tax + insurance + maintenance + closing costs)
  • renter's cost R(P) = cumulative rent paid (+ tenant insurance) on housing:rent
  • break-even condition (decided): G(P) ≥ −R(P), i.e. owning has cost no more than renting. The literal "gain ≥ rent" was rejected because it counts the rent twice.
  • Evaluation: tallies plus a goal evaluated every month by the stepping stage (ADR-0027, Proposed):
    • tally ownerNetGain (in the buy scenario) = Derived: (VALUE(home) − BALANCE(mortgage)) − TagSum(housing:buy, outflows)
    • tally rentSpent = TagSum(housing:rent, outflows), in the rent scenario
    • goal REACH: ownerNetGain + rentSpent ≥ 0, which reports the break-even period
  • buy and rent run in lockstep as one scenario set (ADR-0028). The break-even goal is a set-level goal: buy.ownerNetGain + rent.rentSpent ≥ 0.
