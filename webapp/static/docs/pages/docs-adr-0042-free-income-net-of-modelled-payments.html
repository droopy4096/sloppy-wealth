ADR-0042: Free income can be declared net of payments the engine models

  • Status: Deferred (2026-11-07)
  • Date: 2026-11-05
  • Builds on: ADR-0040 (crude input is first-class), ADR-0029 (derived loan payments)

Context

Users naturally state "free income": what is left after paying everything, including mortgages. The engine also derives and debits mortgage payments itself (ADR-0029), so entering free income as-is counts the mortgages twice. Asking the user to add the payments back by hand defeats the point of crude input, and breaks when payments change (renewals, a sale).

Decision (proposed)

  • An income CashFlow can declare netOf: a list of loans, costs or cash flows that its amount has already been reduced by.
  • Each period, the engine credits the amount plus the current amounts of the netOf items that are active in that period. The engine then debits those items normally, so the net effect equals the stated free income.
  • When a netOf item ends (e.g. a loan is paid off or settled by a sale), the add-back ends too, so the free income rises by the payment no longer made, which is what happens in reality.

Alternatives considered

  • Authoring tooling grossing the figure up by hand: it would have to duplicate the payment logic and goes stale at renewals and sales.
  • Not modelling mortgages when free income is used: loses balances, equity and the sale settlement.

Consequences

  • The add-back amounts are recorded as movements tagged with the income, so they stay visible and auditable.
  • netOf items must belong to the same scenario; this is validated on input.

Deferral (2026-11-07)

  • Deferred, not rejected. ADR-0044 (income-less mode) covers the case with no free money, and the scenarios at hand have full income and expense data, so nothing needs netOf now.
  • Revisit when a scenario states a partial "free income" alongside modelled loans.
