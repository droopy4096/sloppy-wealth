ADR-0063: Cash balance reconciliation item

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-11
  • Relates to: ADR-0011 (holdings, implicit cash), ADR-0033 (revaluation; C-P16 market adjustment of accounts), ADR-0062 (cash flow adjustment)

Context

A plan that starts in the past (the owner's starts in 2019) drifts from the real bank balance: actual spending, gifts and one-off costs are not all modelled. Today the cash balance can only be corrected with a one-month income or expense whose amount must be worked out by hand and goes stale when earlier items change.

Decision

A cashBalance item (balanceDate, balance) states the actual cash balance at the end of that month. The engine sets cash to it; the difference is a CASH_RECONCILIATION movement from or to outside the model.

Mechanics (defaults; not separate owner decisions)

  • Applied after the month's tax step, before the month's results row, so the row shows the stated balance.
  • Not income, not an expense, not taxable; it changes no other holding.
  • Several items follow each other (e.g. one per statement); in one month the last in item order wins.
  • The date must be within the horizon. Also applies in income-less mode.
  • Visible in the ledger and the event log (the difference, and the balance before).
  • Investment accounts are not covered (C-P16, decided, built separately).

Alternatives considered

  • marketAdjustment on cash: cash does not change value; the name and its percent option do not fit.
  • One-month income or expense (no change): manual and goes stale.

Consequences

  • Plans can be anchored to real statements; the gap between plan and reality is shown.
