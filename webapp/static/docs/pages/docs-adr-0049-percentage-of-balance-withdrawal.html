ADR-0049: Withdrawal as a percentage of the balance, reset yearly

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-07
  • Relates to: ADR-0039 (even drawdown), ADR-0035 (withdrawal tax), ADR-0013 (yearly step on the anniversary)

Context

Funds (RRSP, TFSA, investments) could be drawn by a fixed amount or by an even drawdown over the remaining horizon (ADR-0039). Planning also needs "withdraw X% a year", where the income follows the fund's performance.

Decision

  • A new cash flow, PercentWithdrawal: from : Account, start : Period, end : Period [0..1], annualRate : RateGenerator.
  • On the start period and on each anniversary of it, the yearly amount is set to balance at the opening of that period × annual rate; it is paid as 1/12 each month (ADR-0021) until the next anniversary.
  • Amounts are gross; tax applies as for any withdrawal (ADR-0035). A month's payment is capped at the balance (ADR-0015).
  • The yearly reset on the anniversary is the same cadence as indexing (ADR-0013 amendment), so the model has one rhythm for yearly changes. RRIF minimum withdrawals follow the same pattern (rate × balance at the start of the year).

Alternatives considered

  • Recalculated every month: income changes every month.
  • The classic "4% rule" (a fixed amount from the starting balance, indexed to inflation): can be added later as an option; not needed now.

Consequences

  • A percentage withdrawal never fully empties the fund; an even drawdown (ADR-0039) does.
  • Retirement income tallies (ADR-0037) include these withdrawals by tag, like any other withdrawal.
