ADR-0073: Property break-even and rent equivalent

  • Status: Proposed (design agreed 2026-11-18, Q-49)
  • Date: 2026-11-18
  • Relates to: ADR-0024 / ADR-0047 (early-closure penalty), ADR-0031 / ADR-0034 (sale), ADR-0067 (associated expenses and costs), ADR-0053 (output model)

Context

The owner wants a calculator per property: each month, what would a sale bring compared with everything paid for the property so far? Two figures:

  • break-even: net proceeds of a hypothetical sale that month (market value − selling costs − mortgage balance − early-closure penalty) minus the net investment (all paid for the property so far); positive = ahead;
  • rent equivalent: (net investment − net proceeds) / months owned, the money "lost" per month, i.e. the rent that would have cost the same; it should turn negative over time.

Decision

  1. Source (Q-49 a, owner 2026-11-18): from the run. The figures are read from the run's ledger and holdings (what was actually paid for the property each month, its value and mortgage balance), with a hypothetical sale computed beside it every month; the run is not changed. A page like Run, not a builder card figure.
  2. Net investment (Q-49 b, owner 2026-11-18): two variants, shown as distinct columns (with break-even and rent equivalent for each):
    • ownership costs: down payment (price − mortgage advance), closing costs (associated costs at the start), every mortgage payment, prepayment and lump sum, penalties, improvements, and the property's (and its mortgages') associated expenses, except those flagged as also paid when renting (e.g. utilities: a new associated-expense flag);
    • all linked costs: the same with every associated expense and cost, flagged or not. No forgone return on the down payment.
  3. Before the horizon (Q-49 c, owner 2026-11-18): start at the horizon. For a property bought before the horizon start, the equity at the start (value − mortgage balance) is the initial investment and months owned count from the horizon start; the past is not estimated. (A property bought inside the horizon counts from its purchase, with its down payment and closing costs.)
  4. Early-closure penalty (Q-49 d, owner 2026-11-18): a formula, used by both. A mortgage, and each renewal for its own term, may give termMonths (term length from its start) and a penalty method: a flat amount, a % of the interest left in the term (the schedule's interest from the month of sale to the term's end), or N months' interest (3 is common in Canada). After the term ends there is no penalty. The break-even's hypothetical sale uses it every month; a real sale (or lump-sum payoff) uses it when its own salePenalty / penalty is blank. Files without a term give the same results as before. This amends ADR-0047.
  5. Selling costs (Q-49 e, owner 2026-11-18): the property's associated costs at its end (ADR-0067), applied to each month's market value (a rate) or as they are (an amount). Nothing new to enter; the page notes when a property has none.
  6. Presentation (Q-49 f, owner 2026-11-18): a Break-even page beside Run (per holding, ledger), with a property picker and a link from each property card. At the top, the month each variant first breaks even and the current figures; a line chart of break-even and rent equivalent (both variants) over the months; below, the monthly table: months owned, market value, selling costs, mortgage balance, penalty, net proceeds, then net investment, break-even and rent equivalent for each variant.

Alternatives considered

  • Estimated from the items like the builder cards: approximate (prepayments, adjustments, triggers).
  • Both: more to build for little gain.
  • Net investment with the forgone return on the down payment and extra costs: one more assumption (a rate); not now.
  • Before the horizon: estimating the past from the items with an override field, or a manual figure only.
  • Penalty formula for the break-even only (real sales keep ADR-0047's manual amount); or only the flat and remaining-interest methods.
  • New property fields for selling costs: duplicates associated costs (ADR-0067).
  • Presentation as a table only, or as columns on the Run page (too wide with several properties).

Consequences

  • New fields: termMonths, penaltyMethod with penaltyAmount / penaltyRate / penaltyMonths on mortgages and renewals; alsoWhenRenting on associated expenses. Diagram 02 v0.56; glossary: Break-even, Rent equivalent.
  • A real sale's penalty changes only for files that give a term and no manual penalty.

Implementation notes (2026-11-18, poc/sm-break-even)

  • Field names: penaltyMethod flat / interestLeft / monthsInterest with penaltyAmount / penaltyRate (a fraction, % in the form) / penaltyMonths (rather than one penaltyValue, so the rate can be a percentage field).
  • The penalty is computed by the loan (Mortgage.penalty_at, term in effect: its own or its latest renewal's); the run records it on the loan's holdings points (closingPenalty, only for loans with a term) for the break-even, and uses it for a sale or lump-sum payoff without a manual penalty. Renewals keep their manual penalty for breaking the previous term.
  • state_machine/break_even.py reads the ledger and holdings; the page is /run/break-even (web/break_even_draw.py draws the chart; the rent-equivalent panel is scaled from the 12th month, as the first months' figures are huge by nature). Amounts are nominal.
  • Owner's request 2026-11-18: the chart was not helpful and is dropped (web/break_even_draw.py removed); the page is the summary and the monthly table.
  • Owner's choice 2026-11-18: short column titles, each with its formula as a tooltip: Month, Owned (mo), Value, Sale costs, Loan balance, Penalty, Net proceeds, then Invested, Gain and Rent eq. for "owner" costs and for "all" costs (the summary uses the same words).
  • Owner's request 2026-11-18: one click from each property: a chart icon (purple) with the other icons on the property's card and on its row in both builder views.
