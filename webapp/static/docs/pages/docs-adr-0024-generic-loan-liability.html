ADR-0024: A generic Loan liability, with Mortgage as a specialisation

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Vehicle purchases may be financed. Only Mortgage existed as a liability.

Decision

  • Hierarchy: Liability → Loan → Mortgage.
  • Loan: amortising, an interest rate generator, amortisation in months, optional securedBy asset, prepayment options, early closure penalty rule. Default compounding MONTHLY.
  • Mortgage: a loan secured by real estate, with a TermScheduleRate and SEMI_ANNUAL compounding by default (ADR-0019).
  • Settlement on sale (ADR-0018 step 4) applies to every loan secured by the sold asset, not only mortgages.

Consequences

  • A car loan is a Loan with securedBy = the vehicle. Selling the vehicle settles the loan.
