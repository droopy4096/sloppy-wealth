ADR-0074: Vehicle cost of ownership per month

  • Status: Proposed (owner's decision 2026-11-18, Q-50)
  • Date: 2026-11-18
  • Relates to: ADR-0073 (property break-even: the same approach), ADR-0067 (associated expenses and costs)

Context

After the property break-even (ADR-0073), the owner wants a similar calculator for vehicles: each month, everything paid for the vehicle so far, less its value, spread over the months owned: the total cost of ownership per month.

Decision (Q-50, owner 2026-11-18: "same as properties")

  1. From the run, like ADR-0073: per month owned, from the ledger and holdings.
  2. Paid so far = the purchase price plus every associated expense and cost of the vehicle (fuel, insurance, maintenance, repairs) paid up to that month.
  3. Value = its market value at the month's end less its selling costs (associated costs at its end, ADR-0067), if any.
  4. Cost per month = (paid so far − value) / months owned.
  5. Bought before the horizon: its value at the horizon start counts as already paid, and months count from the horizon start. A real sale ends the table.
  6. Page like the break-even one: a summary (cost per month at the base month and at the end) and a monthly table with short titles and tooltips (Month, Owned (mo), Value, Sale costs, Paid so far, Cost/mo), opened by the chart icon on each vehicle's card and row. One figure (no owner/all split).

Alternatives considered

  • Also a depreciation-only figure ((purchase − value) / months) beside the total.
  • The value without selling costs (strictly the formula as first stated).

Consequences

  • New view state_machine/vehicle_cost.py, POST /api/view/vehicle-cost, page /run/vehicle-cost. No new fields; results unchanged.
