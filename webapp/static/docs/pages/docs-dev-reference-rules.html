Rules and calculators

A rule is a calculator that emitters and events call. Rules subclass base.Rule, hold no scenario state (state they need lives on the household), and use the formulas in state_machine/arith.py. Rule modules live in state_machine/rules/; the jurisdiction-specific rules (tax treatment of accounts, contribution limits) live in state_machine/jurisdictions/ (jurisdictions.md).

TaxRule

One flat rate on taxable income less deductions, per person, every month (ADR-0017, ADR-0048, ADR-0061).

  • Inputs: each person's TaxAccumulator (household.tax_of(person)), reset at the start of each month (Household.begin_month):
    • taxable: added by add_taxable (taxable income, pension payments, taxed returns, taxed withdrawals);
    • deductions: added by add_deduction (deductible contributions and transfers, pension contributions);
    • extra_tax: tax at an account's own withdrawalTaxRate, kept unrounded (add_own_rate_tax);
    • pool: unused deductions carried forward; never reset.
  • Step (TaxRule.person_step): available = deductions + pool; used = min(available, max(0, taxable)); pool = available − used; incomeTax = money(rate × (max(0, taxable) − used)); total = incomeTax + money(extra_tax). Tax is never negative; no cash credit.
  • Rate: person.current_tax_rate: the person's effectiveTaxRate (else the scenario's), switching to the retirement rate once the person's Retirement event has run.
  • Output: one TAX movement per person (source: person id when there are several), and the month's detail in household.tax_detail (see outputs.md).
  • Wiring: TaxEmitter (Order.TAX, scenario level) emits a TaxStepEvent every month. It is registered by the scenario setup hook setup_retirement_and_tax in events/retirement.py.

IndexingRule

A flow's yearly step-up (ADR-0013), with cash-flow adjustments (ADR-0062).

IndexingRule(rate, anchor, divisor=1, steps=None)
IndexingRule.for_item(item, inflation, anchor, divisor=1, steps=None)  # rate from item
rule.amount(base, month) -> Decimal                                       # rounded to cents
  • rate: from arith.indexing_rate(item, inflation): inflation → the scenario's annualInflation, custom → indexRate, else 0.
  • anchor: the month index the amount is stated for (the item's start, or the parent's start for associated items).
  • divisor: arith.PER[per]: 1 (month), 3 (quarter), 12 (year).
  • steps: [(month, new base, divisor)] from flows/adjustment.steps_for; the last step at or before month replaces base, divisor and anchor.
  • amount = arith.indexed(base, rate, month − anchor, divisor) = money(base × (1 + rate)^((month − anchor) // 12) / divisor).

Pensions do not use it: Pension.payment steps on the anniversary of the base date (dollarsOf) with the same expression. Percent withdrawals and percentage associated items use their own yearly re-set (PercentStrategy, YearlyPercent, percent_of).

CapitalGainsRule

A deliberate no-op: no tax on gains from selling a property or vehicle (known gap C-M8, ADR-0017).

  • CapitalGainsRule.on_sale(household, asset, proceeds, source) returns None, records nothing, adds no taxable income.
  • RealAsset.capital_gains = NO_CAPITAL_GAINS (the shared instance); Sale.apply calls it after the SALE movement. A future capital-gains treatment plugs in here.

Closure penalty

The early-closure penalty of a mortgage term (ADR-0073), Mortgage.penalty_at(month) with the ClosureTerm of the term in effect (the mortgage's own, or the latest renewal's):

penaltyMethodPenalty
flatpenaltyAmount
interestLeftpenaltyRate × the schedule's interest over the months left in the term (Mortgage.interest_left, no prepayments)
monthsInterestpenaltyMonths × balance × monthly rate

0 after the term ends, without a term, or when nothing is owed. Used by Sale.apply (sale without salePenalty), ClosurePenalty (lump-sum payoff without penalty) and Mortgage.report_extra (holdings field closingPenalty = penalty if closed after that month).
