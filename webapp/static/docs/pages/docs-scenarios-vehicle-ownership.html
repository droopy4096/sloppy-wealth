Scenario set: vehicle ownership (new vs lease vs used)

Three competing scenarios over the same horizon, compared against each other (ADR-0006). Every vehicle-related object is tagged vehicle (ADR-0023). Prices are entered including HST. Insurance and registration are optional FixedCosts.

Common to all three

ElementTypeDetails
HorizonHorizonsame start and end in all three, length T years
BankAccount (TAXABLE)pays for everything; has a return rate, so money spent on the car shows up as investment growth forgone
InflationRateGenerator (untagged, shared)for real-dollar comparison

Shifting the purchase: shift tag vehicle. The vehicle, the purchase, the loan, the cost and the warranty rate segments move together.

Comparison

  • Primary: net worth at the end of the horizon (real dollars). An owned car is counted at its residual value; a lease leaves nothing.
  • Secondary: total cost by tag vehicle, i.e. the sum of all tagged movements.
