ADR-0056: ResidenceSwitchEvent moves the principal-residence designation

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-09
  • Relates to: ADR-0017 (capital gains, principal residence flag), ADR-0034 (sale), ADR-0007 (events), ADR-0018 (order of steps); CRITIQUE.md C-P13

Context

The principal-residence flag was all-or-nothing per property. Owning two homes over time (e.g. a first house, then a second one) needs the designation to move between them, and Canada exempts only part of the gain when a property was designated for some years.

Decision

  • New event ResidenceSwitchEvent (date, to : RealEstate). From its month the target property is the principal residence and no other property is.
  • RealEstate.principalResidence gives the initial designation, at purchase or horizon start. Switch events change it over time. At most one property is designated at any moment (validation error otherwise).
  • The event's target must be owned at its date.
  • Designated years of a property: calendar years in which it holds the designation on 31 December, plus the year of its sale if it holds the designation at the sale. Owned years: calendar years in which it is owned at any time.
  • At sale, exempt share = min(1, (1 + designated years) ÷ owned years) (the CRA "+1" formula). Taxable gain = gain × (1 − exempt share) × inclusion rate (ADR-0017).
  • The engine does not optimise the designation; the user places the switches.

Alternatives considered

  • A list of designated year ranges per property: same result, harder to keep exclusive.
  • A user-entered exempt share: simpler, but the user computes the formula.

Consequences

  • A property never designated is fully taxable; one designated for its whole ownership is fully exempt, as before.
  • The year-end rule plus "+1" covers the overlap year of a move.
  • For a property bought before the horizon, owned years count from its purchase date; the initial flag is its designation for those years.
