ADR-0022: A variable cost is a percentage of the asset's acquisition price

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Costs such as maintenance are "% of the asset". Basing them on the current depreciated value makes vehicle maintenance fall as the car ages, the opposite of reality.

Decision

  • Variable cost (monthly) = rate(P) × acquisition price / 12, where the rate comes from the cost's rate generator (e.g. X% during the warranty, then X'%).
  • The acquisition price is the purchase price of the asset, i.e. what it cost to acquire. It is a fixed input. It is not the cost basis, which grows as costs are paid (ADR-0014). Using the cost basis would compound costs on themselves.
  • The cost's indexing (ADR-0013) still applies on top, e.g. an inflation-indexed maintenance cost.

Consequences

  • Asset gains acquisitionPrice (input). costBasis is the running metric: acquisition price + costs paid.
  • For long horizons such as houses, a variable cost should normally be inflation-indexed, or the percentage of an old purchase price will understate future costs.
