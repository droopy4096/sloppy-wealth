ADR-0007: Monthly time model on real dates with independent, shiftable events

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Mortgages, rent, salaries and contributions happen monthly. Strategies mostly differ in when things happen, and the user needs to move them in time easily.

Decision

  • The engine steps in monthly periods on real calendar dates.
  • Decisions and events (start of double mortgage payments, purchase, sale, lump-sum prepayment, retirement, ...) are first-class, dated objects.
  • Every date is independent and absolute: there is no anchoring of one date to another.
  • Shifting is handled by tooling: operations that shift one or several objects, possibly of different types, by an offset, followed by a recomputation of the projection.

Alternatives considered

  • Yearly periods: too coarse for mortgage amortisation and prepayment.
  • Relative time ("month N"): makes tax years, ages and calendar events awkward.
  • Anchored or relative dates with a dependency graph: rejected, because it adds complexity and the time-shift tooling covers the same need explicitly.

Consequences

  • Every dated object needs a common way to be shifted (e.g. a shared "time-shiftable" interface), which the shift tooling works with.
  • Yearly concerns (tax year, annual limits) are handled as events at year boundaries inside the monthly stepping.
