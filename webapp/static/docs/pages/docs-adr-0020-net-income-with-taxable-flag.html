ADR-0020: Income is entered net, with an optional taxable flag

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

The simple tax model (ADR-0004, ADR-0017) has no income-tax brackets. Salary is usually known as take-home pay, while other income such as rent is received gross and taxed later.

Decision

  • Income cash flows are entered net (after tax) by default.
  • An income cash flow can be flagged taxable. The engine then taxes it at the flat effective tax rate in the tax step (ADR-0018 step 7).

Consequences

  • Salary is entered as take-home pay. Rent, and any other gross income, is flagged taxable.
  • Expenses that reduce taxable rental income (deductions) are not modelled.

Amendment (2026-11-07): defaults differ between files and entry screens

  • In JSON, an income cash flow without taxable is after tax (taxable = false), the neutral default (ADR-0040): omitting the flag never invents a tax.
  • Authoring screens pre-fill new income as taxable (gross), because salaries are usually known gross. This is a UI default only; pensions already known net and "free income" are entered with taxable switched off.
  • Tax on taxable income is still computed in the tax step (ADR-0018 step 7), together with every other tax. The PoC deducts it as income arrives (withholding); within a month the result is the same, and the PoC will be aligned later.
