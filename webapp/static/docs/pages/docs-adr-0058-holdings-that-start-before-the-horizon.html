ADR-0058: Assets and loans that start before the horizon

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-09
  • Relates to: ADR-0025 (purchase), ADR-0029 (mortgage schedule), ADR-0043 (renewals), ADR-0057 (accounts before the horizon); CRITIQUE.md C-M5

Context

Assets bought before the horizon were already supported in the PoC (grown from their purchase date), but loans had to start inside the horizon. The only workaround, a new mortgage at horizon start, booked a fake loan advance and overstated net worth by the principal. A plan started from today makes every existing mortgage pre-horizon.

Decision

  • Assets bought before the horizon start enter at their value grown from the purchase date at their value rate (and any pre-horizon improvements or market adjustments). No cash is booked for the purchase. (Records existing PoC behaviour.)
  • Loans that start before the horizon are replayed: the engine runs the schedule from the loan's own start, with its entered terms and renewals (ADR-0043), up to the horizon start, without booking any cash (no advance, no payments). The horizon starts with the replayed balance, rate, term and remaining amortisation.
  • Optional balanceAtHorizonStart: overrides the replayed balance to match a statement (e.g. unentered prepayments). The scheduled payment is then recalculated from that balance over the remaining amortisation.
  • Accounts are not replayed (ADR-0057): their opening value is the balance at horizon start.

Consequences

  • No fake advance; net worth at horizon start reflects the real debt.
  • Lump-sum prepayments dated before the horizon are applied in the replay; without them the override is the way to match a statement.
