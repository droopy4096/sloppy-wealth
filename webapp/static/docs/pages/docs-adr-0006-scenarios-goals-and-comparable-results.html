ADR-0006: Scenarios, goals and comparable projection results as the core concepts

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

This is a planning tool. The user builds several alternative strategies and compares their outcomes to choose the best one. Rent vs buy is one example of such a comparison.

Decision

  • A Scenario is a named set of assets, liabilities, cash flows, assumptions (rate series) and dated events. Each scenario defines its own horizon.
  • A scenario may define Goals, i.e. target conditions such as "accumulate $1M in retirement funds" or "reach $3k monthly pension income". Goals are evaluated against the projection.
  • Running a scenario produces a ProjectionResult in a standard structure (timeline and metrics) that can be compared with others.
  • Scenario comparison is a core capability. Specialised calculators such as rent vs buy are expressed as comparisons between scenarios, not as separate code paths.

Alternatives considered

  • Separate standalone calculators: rejected, because their logic would be duplicated and their results could not be compared.

Consequences

  • Results from scenarios with different horizons must still be comparable, which needs rules for aligning or truncating the timelines.
  • Goal evaluation needs a design of its own: whether a goal was reached, when it was reached, and the shortfall if not.
  • Comparing scenarios with different horizons is still open (see open-questions Q-04).

Amendment (2026-11-04): goals are observed, not solved

  • The engine watches goals during a projection and reports when each goal is met (the period/date it is first met). An unmet goal is reported as such.
  • The engine does not search for inputs that would meet a goal. Solving or optimising for goals is the job of a possible separate consumer engine that calls this one repeatedly. That engine is out of scope.
  • Consequence: goals act like observers on the projection timeline. The engine's API must stay cheap to call repeatedly and must not depend on hidden state, so a future optimiser can drive it.
