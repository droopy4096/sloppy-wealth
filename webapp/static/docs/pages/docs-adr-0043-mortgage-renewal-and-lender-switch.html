ADR-0043: Mortgage renewal and switching lenders at a term boundary

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-07
  • Amends: ADR-0019 (MortgageTerm), ADR-0029 (payment recalculation)

Context

A mortgage is renewed at the end of each term, usually at a new rate. At renewal the borrower can also move the mortgage to another institution, which typically means a new amortisation period as well as a new rate. Porting a mortgage to another property is not needed (the sale closes the loan, ADR-0034).

Decision

  • A renewal is the start of a new MortgageTerm in the mortgage's TermScheduleRate (ADR-0019). A term has a start, a length and a rate.
  • A term may also carry an optional amortizationMonths. When given, the remaining amortisation is reset to it at the term start. When omitted, the amortisation simply continues (remaining = original − months elapsed).
  • Switching to another institution is modelled as a renewal whose term sets a new rate and a new amortisation. The lender's identity is not modelled; the mortgage stays the same holding with the same balance, so goals and tallies attached to it carry on.
  • At each term start the engine recalculates the scheduled payment (ADR-0029) from the opening balance of that month, the new monthly rate and the remaining amortisation. The new rate applies to that month's interest.
  • Switching fees (legal, appraisal, discharge) are optional one-off costs, modelled with a OneOffTransfer (ADR-0025) in the switch month. A switch at a term boundary has no early closure penalty. A switch inside a term is an early closure: the penalty rule applies (its formula is still to be defined).
  • The final scheduled payment of the amortisation clears any rounding remainder.

Alternatives considered

  • Model a switch as closing the old mortgage and opening a new one (a new loan funding a lump-sum payoff): works with existing pieces, but splits one debt into two holdings, breaks goals attached to the mortgage, and makes the common case clumsy.
  • Allow only a new rate at renewal: cannot express a lender switch with a new amortisation.

Consequences

  • MortgageTerm gains amortizationMonths [0..1] in the domain model.
  • Increasing the balance at a switch (refinancing to take equity out) is not covered; it would still be a new loan. Revisit if needed.
  • Prepayments made before a renewal lower the recalculated payment at that renewal, because the payment is derived from the balance then (consistent with ADR-0029).

Amendment (2026-11-09): renewal may keep the payment (C-M7)

  • MortgageTerm gains keepPayment : bool = false. When true, the scheduled payment before the renewal (excluding extra monthly prepayments) is kept; the new rate applies, and the remaining amortisation is derived from the balance, rate and payment. Time saved by prepayments is kept.
  • keepPayment and amortizationMonths are mutually exclusive (validation error).
  • If the kept payment does not cover the new rate's interest within the original remaining amortisation, the payment is recalculated as usual and the result warns.
  • Default false keeps the behaviour above: prepayment savings become a lower payment.
