ADR-0051: Contributions are cash flows into an account, with an optional employer match

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-07
  • Relates to: ADR-0012 (cash flows), ADR-0021 (per month/quarter/year), ADR-0048 (deduction)

Context

Saving into an RRSP, TFSA or investment account needs a contribution level, and group RRSPs often add an employer match.

Decision

  • A contribution is a CashFlow from the operating account (or another account) into the target account: amount, per, start, optional end, indexing. No new engine concept; authoring offers a dedicated form.
  • A contribution may have employerMatch : Decimal [0..1] (a fraction, ADR-0045). Each period the employer adds employerMatch × the contribution paid into the same account, from outside the model. It follows the contribution automatically when the contribution changes, is indexed or stops.
  • The employer share is neither taxable income nor a deduction (ADR-0048 amendment).
  • Matching caps (e.g. "up to 5% of salary") are not modelled; the user sets the contribution so that the match is right.

Alternatives considered

  • A separate inflow entered by hand: goes stale when the contribution changes.

Consequences

  • In income-less mode (ADR-0044) the contribution is covered from outside and the match still lands in the account.
