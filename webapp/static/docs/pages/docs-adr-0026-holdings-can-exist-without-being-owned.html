ADR-0026: A holding can exist without being owned (lease, then buyout)

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

For a lease, the buyout price at the end should follow from the vehicle's depreciation (MSRP depreciated to the lease end), not from a hand-entered number.

Decision

  • Holding gains owned : bool (default true).
  • A holding that is not owned is still projected (its value follows its value rate each month), but it:
    • is excluded from net worth and from global goals,
    • carries no costs and cannot be sold.
  • A PurchaseEvent on an existing holding that is not owned transfers ownership. Its price defaults to the holding's current projected value in that period. From then on the holding is owned and counts normally.
  • If the lease ends without a buyout, nothing more is needed: the vehicle that is not owned remains excluded.

Alternatives considered

  • A price derived by a formula on the buyout event: a one-off concept used by a single case.
  • A hand-entered residual value: drifts when the depreciation assumption changes.

Consequences

  • PurchaseEvent.price becomes optional: required for a new holding, defaulting to current value for one that is not owned.
  • Lease-related costs (payments, maintenance) are modelled as cash flows tagged with the lease, not as costs of the holding.

Amendment (2026-11-04): reset on ownership transfer

  • When a purchase transfers ownership of a holding that was not owned, the holding's acquisition price and cost basis are reset to the buyout price. From then on, variable costs (ADR-0022) and capital gains (ADR-0017) are based on what was actually paid, the same as buying a used vehicle.
