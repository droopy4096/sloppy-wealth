ADR-0016: A reference birth date, with ages as a way of entering dates

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

Plans are naturally stated in ages ("retire at 60", "plan until 80"), while the engine works on absolute dates (ADR-0007).

Decision

  • The economic unit has a reference birth date: the person whose age the plan is framed around.
  • Ages are a way of entering dates. Authoring tooling converts "age 60" into an absolute period when the value is entered. The scenario stores only absolute dates.

Alternatives considered

  • Storing ages and resolving them in the engine: that brings back anchored dates, which ADR-0007 rejected.
  • No ages at all: inconvenient for retirement planning.

Consequences

  • Changing the reference birth date does not move dates already entered. Time-shift tooling can move them if needed (ADR-0007).
  • Views can still display ages next to periods, derived from the reference birth date.
  • With a family as the economic unit, only one person's age frames the plan; consistent with ADR-0002.
