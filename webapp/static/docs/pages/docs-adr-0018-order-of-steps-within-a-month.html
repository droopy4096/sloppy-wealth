ADR-0018: Order of steps within a month

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04

Context

The monthly stepping stage (ADR-0010) runs a fixed sequence of steps for each period. The order defines the accounting semantics: what earns interest, which debits fall short first (ADR-0015), and whether a property sold this month still incurs this month's costs.

Decision (revision 2)

For each period P:

  1. Open: carry closed balances and values from P−1.
  2. Resolve: rates for P (rate generators, ADR-0019) and cash flow amounts for P.
  3. Inflows first: credit income cash flows, so there are no false shortfalls.
  4. Events: sales, purchases and lump-sum prepayments, applied after income.
  5. Debits in fixed priority: liability payments, then costs, then expenses, then transfers and contributions, then withdrawals. Each is capped at available funds; shortfalls are recorded.
  6. Growth and interest: revalue assets, apply account returns, accrue liability interest. Interest and returns are computed on the opening balance (interest in arrears, as banks do). A payment or lump sum made in P reduces interest from P+1. This keeps the projection reconcilable with real statements and the standard amortisation formula.
  7. Tax at the flat effective rate (same capping rule).
  8. Close: snapshot balances, values, cost basis and cumulative shortfall.

Every money movement is rounded to 2 dp, half-even, as it happens (ADR-0009).

Revision 1 (rejected) applied events at the start of the month and credited income after them. The user found that counter-intuitive: a payment made in a month must show on the mortgage in that month, and income must arrive first so that no false shortfalls appear.

Alternatives considered

  • Events at the end of the month: a sold property would incur that month's costs and interest, which makes settlement more complicated.
  • Interest on the balance after payments: feels more natural, but understates interest by about a month on every payment compared with the bank.
  • User-defined debit priority: more flexible, but more to configure. Could be added later.

Consequences

  • Inflows come before events and debits, so this month's income can pay this month's purchase, bills and payments.
  • Payments made in P always show in P's closing balance.
  • Rates are converted to monthly using each holding's compounding convention (ADR-0019).

Amendments (2026-11-04)

  • Step 5: scheduled loan payments are derived by the engine (ADR-0029) and paid first within the liability-payments group.
  • Step 7a (new): sweep rules run after tax and before close (ADR-0030).
  • Steps 9–10 (new): update tallies, then evaluate scenario-level and set-level goals, after every scenario in the set has closed the period (ADR-0027, ADR-0028).
  • Step 4: sale settlement deducts selling costs (ADR-0031).
  • Step 4 (2026-11-05): revaluation events (ADR-0033), and the full sale settlement order including negative equity (ADR-0034).
