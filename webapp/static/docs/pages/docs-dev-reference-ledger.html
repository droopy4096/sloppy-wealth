Ledger movements

Every balance change in a run is one ledger line, recorded by Household.move() (state_machine/household.py) into household.ledger (state_machine/ledger.py, ADR-0053). Nothing else changes a balance.

Line format

run_detailed(scenario)["ledger"] is a list of dicts, in the order the movements happened, with keys in this order:

KeyTypeMeaning
month"YYYY-MM"Month of the movement.
kindstringOne of the kinds below.
fromholding id or nullWhere the money comes from. null = outside the model.
toholding id or nullWhere it goes. null = outside the model.
amountDecimal (string in the API)Rounded half-even to cents. Never 0: a movement that rounds to 0 is not recorded. Valuation kinds may be negative.
sourceitem id, person id or nullThe item that caused it (see each kind).

Holding ids: "cash", or the id of a property, vehicle, mortgage, account or pension item.

move(kind, src, dst, amount, source) subtracts from src and adds to dst. A mortgage is a liability: its balance is positive when owed, so money from a mortgage raises the balance (advance, interest) and money to it lowers the balance (payments). The sign is flipped in one place, Aggregate._apply (base.py).

The "Cash" column below is the effect on cash: + into cash, − out of cash. "Tax" says whether the movement feeds the tax step (rules.md). The ledger page (POST /api/view/ledger) adds a signed cash field per line the same way.

Old kinds no longer produced: MAINTENANCE and SELLING_COST (older files' vehicle maintenance and selling costs now give EXPENSE and COST lines, see upgrade.py).

Income and spending

INCOME

  • outside → cash. Source: the income, associatedIncome or associatedOneOff item.
  • flows/income_expense.py Income. Step INCOME.
  • Tax: added to the owner's taxable income when the item (for associated items, the parent) is taxable; counted as earned income when earnedIncome.

EXPENSE

  • cash → outside. Source: the expense or associatedExpense item.
  • flows/income_expense.py Expense. Step EXPENSE. Not deductible.

COST

  • cash → outside. Source: the associatedCost item.
  • flows/associated.py Cost. Step EXPENSE. Not deductible.

Properties and vehicles

PURCHASE

  • cash → asset, the price. Source: the asset id.
  • events/purchase.py Purchase. Step ACQUIRE, purchase month. Not taxed.

REVALUATION

  • outside → asset or pension. May be negative. Source: the asset id, the improvement id, or the pension id.
  • events/purchase.py Revaluation (marketValue − price at purchase; valueAdded − cost after an improvement); holdings/pension.py PensionRevalued (to the pension's value, step REVALUE_PENSIONS, every month). Not taxed.

APPRECIATION

  • outside → asset: opening value × monthly rate. Negative for a vehicle. Source: the asset id.
  • holdings/real_estate.py Appreciation. Step GROW. Not taxed.

IMPROVEMENT

  • cash → property, the cost. Source: the improvement id.
  • events/improvement.py Improvement. Step IMPROVE. Not taxed.

MARKET_ADJUSTMENT

  • outside → asset: value × percent, or newValue − value. May be negative. Source: the marketAdjustment id.
  • events/adjustment.py MarketAdjustment. Step ADJUST. Not taxed.

SALE_PRICE_DIFFERENCE

  • outside → asset: salePrice − current value, before the sale. May be negative. Source: the sale id (the asset id for an older property saleDate).
  • events/sale.py Sale. Step SELL. Not taxed.

SALE

  • asset → cash, the asset's whole value (after any price difference). Source: the sale id.
  • events/sale.py Sale. Step SELL. Not taxed: the capital-gains rule is a no-op (rules.md).

Mortgages

LOAN_ADVANCE

  • mortgage → cash, the principal. Raises the loan balance. Source: the mortgage id.
  • holdings/mortgage.py Mortgage.advance, event LoanAdvance. Step ACQUIRE.

LOAN_INTEREST

  • mortgage → outside. Raises the balance. Source: the renewal in effect, else the mortgage id.
  • Before the first payment: interest on the principal (accrual). After: interest on the balance before the payment. Mortgage.accrue, Mortgage.charge_interest. Step SERVICE_DEBT. Not deductible.

INTEREST_ADJUSTMENT

  • cash → mortgage (outside → mortgage in expensesCovered mode): the interest accrued before a late first payment, paid in the first payment month. Source: as LOAN_INTEREST.
  • Mortgage.settle_accrued. Step SERVICE_DEBT.

LOAN_PAYMENT

  • cash → mortgage (outside → mortgage in expensesCovered mode): the scheduled payment, capped at what is owed; the last one clears the rest. Source: as LOAN_INTEREST.
  • Mortgage.pay_scheduled. Step SERVICE_DEBT.

PREPAYMENT

  • To the mortgage, capped at the balance. From cash; from outside for an extraPayment in expensesCovered mode or a lumpSum with paidFrom: outside. Source: the extraPayment or lumpSum id.
  • Mortgage.prepay, events ExtraPrepayment, LumpSumPrepayment. Step SERVICE_DEBT.

LOAN_PAYOFF

  • cash → mortgage, the whole balance, when the asset securing it is sold. Source: the sale id.
  • Mortgage.pay_off, event LoanPayoff (events/sale.py). Step SELL.

PENALTY

  • cash → outside. Source: the sale, renewal or lumpSum id.
  • A sale's salePenalty or its loans' term formula (SalePenalty); a renewal's penalty (RenewalPenalty); a lump sum's penalty (LumpSumPenalty) or, for a payoff without one, the term formula (ClosurePenalty). events/sale.py, events/loan.py. Not deductible.

Accounts and pensions

OPENING_BALANCE

  • outside → account: an existing balance not modelled before (account opened inside the horizon, openingFrom: outside). Source: the account id.
  • holdings/account.py AccountOpening. Step ACQUIRE. No tax effect, no limit.

OPENING_TRANSFER

  • cash → account (openingFrom: cash). The whole opening balance: it is the account's current state, not a contribution, so it is not capped by limits and uses no room (ADR-0060 amendment). Source: the account id.
  • holdings/account.py AccountOpening. Step ACQUIRE. Not deducted and no RESP grant (on_contribute is not called).

CONTRIBUTION

  • cash → account (outside → account in expensesCovered mode), capped by limits. Source: the contribution id.
  • InvestmentAccount.accept_contribution. Step CONTRIBUTE.
  • Tax: a deduction of the account's contributor (owner, or spousal RRSP contributor) for deductible types, not in expensesCovered mode.

EMPLOYER_MATCH

  • outside → account: employerMatch × the share contributed, capped by CA room (not by US limits). Source: the contribution id.
  • InvestmentAccount.accept_contribution. Step CONTRIBUTE. Neither income nor a deduction.

PENSION_CONTRIBUTION

  • cash → outside: a contribution whose account is a pension. The pension's value does not change. Not recorded in expensesCovered mode. Source: the contribution id.
  • holdings/pension.py Pension.accept_contribution. Step CONTRIBUTE. Tax: a deduction of the pension's owner.

TRANSFER

  • cash → account, capped by limits; from cash in either income mode. Source: the transfer id.
  • events/transfer.py Transfer. Step TRANSFER. Tax: as CONTRIBUTION (deduction for deductible types, not in expensesCovered mode).

GRANT

  • outside → account: the Canada Education Savings Grant on money put into an RESP (20 %, limited by grant room, $1,000 a year, $7,200 lifetime). Source: the contribution or transfer id.
  • jurisdictions/ca/resp.py pay_grant, called from Canada.on_contribute. Not taxed.

RETURN

  • outside → account: opening balance × monthly rate. May be negative. Source: the account id.
  • holdings/account.py AccountReturn. Step GROW.
  • Tax: taxable income of the owner (a loss counts as 0) when the account type's returns are taxed (CA nonRegistered, US brokerage).

WITHDRAWAL

  • account → cash, capped at the balance. Source: the withdrawal id.
  • flows/withdrawal.py Withdrawal. Step WITHDRAW.
  • Tax: when the type's withdrawals are taxed (CA rrsp, US traditional 401(k) / IRA): taxable income of the owner, or tax at the account's withdrawalTaxRate if set (ownRateTax in the tax detail). TFSA withdrawals give room back the next year.

PENSION_PAYMENT

  • pension → cash. Paid also in expensesCovered mode. Source: the pension id.
  • holdings/pension.py PensionPaid. Step PENSION_PAY. Tax: taxable income of the owner when the pension is taxable (default).

Cash and tax

CASH_RECONCILIATION

  • outside → cash when cash was below the stated balance; cash → outside when above. Source: the cashBalance id.
  • events/cash_balance.py CashReconciled. Order TAX + 50 (after the tax step). Not taxed.

TAX

  • cash → outside: the month's tax, one line per person. Source: null with one person, the person id with several. Not recorded when it rounds to 0.
  • rules/tax.py TaxRule.apply. Step TAX. See rules.md.
