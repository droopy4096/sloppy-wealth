ADR-0030: Sweep rules move surplus cash into investment accounts

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-04
  • Amends: ADR-0018 (adds a sweep step)

Context

Income lands in an operating account, so without a sweep, unspent money earns only that account's rate. In rent vs buy this understates renting, because the opportunity cost of the down payment and of the monthly difference goes missing.

Decision

  • A scenario can define ordered SweepRules: from : Account, buffer : Money, to : Account. Each month, anything in from above buffer moves to to.
  • Sweeps run after tax and before close (new step 7a in ADR-0018), so the period's tax has already been paid before anything is swept.
  • Swept money earns returns in its target account from the next period, because growth is computed on opening balances (ADR-0018).
  • Sweeps go one way only. Covering a shortfall by drawing from investment accounts (a withdrawal waterfall) remains out of scope (ADR-0015).

Consequences

  • In rent vs buy, both scenarios sweep the bank into the same investment account, so the renter's unspent money is invested on equal terms.
  • Contribution limits (e.g. TFSA room) are not enforced (watch item Q-06).
