ADR-0047: The early closure penalty is a manual amount

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-07
  • Amends: ADR-0024 (penalty rule on loans), ADR-0034 (sale settlement), ADR-0043 (lender switch inside a term)

Context

Breaking a mortgage inside its term (a sale, or a switch to another lender) costs a penalty. Canadian lenders charge 3 months' interest, or for fixed rates the greater of that and an interest rate differential whose method varies by lender and depends on future rates. A formula would be either too crude or too speculative.

Decision

  • There is no penalty formula. The loan's earlyClosurePenalty : PenaltyRule is removed.
  • The event that breaks a loan carries an optional penalty : Money, entered by hand (e.g. the lender's quote or an estimate):
    • SaleEvent.penalty: per secured loan settled by the sale (ADR-0034 step 3);
    • a MortgageTerm that starts a switch inside the current term: MortgageTerm.penalty;
    • LumpSumPrepayment.penalty: for a prepayment beyond the lender's privileges.
  • The amount is in dollars of the event date (nominal on that date, as for cash flows, ADR-0013 amendment); it is not indexed.
  • It is paid from the same account as the settlement (the sale proceeds, or the loan's paymentFrom) and recorded as its own movement. Omitted means 0 (ADR-0040).
  • The engine does not check whether the event falls inside a term; entering a penalty is the user's statement that it does.

Alternatives considered

  • 3 months' interest by default: simple, but silently understates fixed-rate penalties.
  • Interest rate differential with a comparison rate: needs lender-specific rules and a forecast of future rates.

Consequences

  • termAt(period) (ADR-0019) is no longer needed for penalties; it remains available.
  • A scenario that breaks a mortgage without entering a penalty shows none.

Amendment (2026-11-09): warning on an in-term closure with no penalty (C-P20)

  • When a secured loan is closed before its current term ends (sale, early renewal or lender switch) and no penalty is entered, the result carries a warning: "<loan> closed <n> months before term end with no penalty entered".
  • No amount and no default are added; the penalty stays manual.

Amendment (2026-11-09): penalty source with outside lump sums (C-M12)

  • A penalty is always paid from the loan's paymentFrom, even when the lump sum itself comes from outside (ADR-0044). Outside money only reduces the loan.

Amendment 2026-11-18 (ADR-0073, Q-49 d)

A mortgage or renewal may now describe its penalty as a formula for its term (termMonths and a method: flat amount, % of the interest left in the term, or N months' interest). A real sale or lump-sum payoff uses it when its own manual penalty is blank; a manual amount still wins. Files without a term are unchanged.
