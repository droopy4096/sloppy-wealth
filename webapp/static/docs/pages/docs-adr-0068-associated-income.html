ADR-0068: Associated income (income that lives with an income or a pension)

  • Status: Proposed (design agreed 2026-11-14, Q-43; implemented on poc/sm-triggers, tag sm-triggers-before-associated-income marks the state before it)
  • Date: 2026-11-14
  • Relates to: ADR-0067 (associated expenses and costs), ADR-0062 (adjustments), ADR-0020 (income gross / net), ADR-0021 (per)

Context

A salary bonus is today an ordinary income item (e.g. "Employer bonus", 2,000 per quarter, ending at retirement). Its total is right, but it does not follow the salary: a raise or an adjustment of the salary does not change it, and its end must be kept in step by hand or by a trigger. Bonuses are usually a share of the base salary, paid while the job lasts. The owner asked for an associated income, as ADR-0067 does for expenses: an income whose lifecycle comes from its parent and whose amount can be looked up from it. Real-world cases with a pension parent are fewer but exist (13th / 14th month pensions, retiree benefit allowances, pension-linked top-ups); a DB bridge benefit (ends at 65) and a survivor pension (starts when the parent ends) do not fit a pure associated item.

Decision

  1. Parents (Q-43a): income and pension. An associated income lives from its parent's start to its end, as an associated expense does (ADR-0067 decision 1).
  2. Amount (Q-43b): a fixed sum or a percentage, as for associated expenses (ADR-0067 decisions 3 and 11): a fixed amount (amount, per), or a yearly rate of the parent's yearly amount (its raises, indexing and adjustments included), taken on the parent's start and on each anniversary.
  3. Payment timing (Q-43c): monthly or in arrears as a lump. paid: monthly (spread evenly, as other flows) or lump: the whole period's amount (per quarter or year) in one payment at the end of each period, i.e. on each quarterly or yearly anniversary of the parent's start, never in its first month (start 2026-08, per quarter: 2026-11, 2027-02, ...). Only full periods are paid: when the parent ends partway through a period, that period's lump is forfeited (as a bonus is when one leaves before its date).
  4. One-offs (Q-43d): a second type, the associated one-off income (working name associatedOneOff), mirroring the associated cost (ADR-0067 decision 5): paid at the parent's start (signing bonus), its end, i.e. its last month (severance), or on its own date while the parent exists (retention bonus); a fixed amount or a percentage of the parent's yearly amount in that month.
  5. Tax and owner (Q-43e): inherited from the parent: taxable, earned income (RRSP room, ADR-0060) and owner (ADR-0061) are the parent's; the forms have no such fields.
  6. Indexing and adjustments (Q-43f): as for associated expenses (ADR-0067 decisions 7 and 8): a fixed amount has the usual indexing from the parent's start and an adjustment gives a new amount; a percentage is not indexed (it follows the parent's amount) and an adjustment gives a new rate (e.g. a bonus target from 10% to 15%).
  7. Migration (Q-43g): none. Nothing in a file says which income is the bonus of which salary; existing bonus incomes keep running as plain incomes and are re-created as associated income by hand.

Alternatives considered

  • A plain income item (status quo): does not follow the salary's amount or end.
  • A "paid as a lump" option on income: fixes payment timing only; the lump timing is part of associated income instead (decision 3).
  • Pro-rated or full lump for a partial last period: rejected; a bonus is usually forfeited when one leaves before its date.
  • Own tax / owner fields: rejected; a bonus is taxed like its salary.
  • Converting bonus incomes on load by name: fragile; may mis-attach.

Consequences

  • Two new item types (associatedIncome, associatedOneOff), mirroring ADR-0067's; the amount basis is extended to pensions. Recurring flows gain a first lump payment rule (in arrears, full periods only), for associated income only.
  • No conversion on load: existing files and the golden suite are unchanged.
  • Domain model: AssociatedIncome and AssociatedOneOffIncome (diagram 02 v0.54).
  • Implemented on poc/sm-triggers (2026-11-14): flows/associated_income.py. Details settled in the implementation: a lump is the rounded sum of the period's unrounded monthly shares (2,000 a quarter pays 2,000, not 3 x 666.67); the last full period's lump is paid in the month after the parent's last month (in arrears), if within the horizon; a one-off at the parent's end needs an endDate within the horizon.
